Yves Saint Laurent, also known as Saint Laurent and YSL, is a French luxury fashion house founded in 1961 by Yves Saint Laurent and his partner, Pierre Bergé. The company specialises in couture, ready-to-wear, leather accessories, and footwear.Its cosmetics line, YSL Beauty, is owned by L'Oréal.Cédric Charbit has been CEO of Yves Saint Laurent since 2024,and Anthony Vaccarello creative director since 2016.In 2024, Yves Saint Laurent reported 2.9 billion euros in sales.
